# What You Can Do with Buddy AI

**Buddy** is your comprehensive AI-powered companion that combines conversational AI, project management, development tools, and smart automation into one powerful platform. Here's everything you can accomplish with Buddy:

## 🤖 AI Conversations & Chat

### Smart AI Conversations
- **Natural Conversations**: Chat with your AI companion about anything - ask questions, seek advice, brainstorm ideas
- **Multiple AI Modes**: Choose between cloud-based API or on-device AI processing
- **Context-Aware Responses**: Buddy remembers your conversation history and provides relevant responses
- **Custom AI Personas**: Create and switch between different AI personalities for specialized assistance

### Chat Features
- **Real-time Messaging**: Instant responses with typing indicators
- **Message History**: All conversations are saved and searchable
- **Rich Media Support**: Share images, files, and multimedia content
- **Voice Integration**: Speak to Buddy naturally (where supported)

### On-Device AI Processing
- **Privacy-First**: Run AI models directly on your device for complete privacy
- **Offline Capability**: Continue conversations without internet connection
- **TensorFlow Lite Models**: Support for efficient mobile AI models (.tflite, .gguf formats)
- **Smart Memory Management**: Automatic optimization for your device's capabilities

## 🌟 Getting Started

### Quick Setup
1. **Install Buddy**: Download from your app store or build from source
2. **Create Account**: Set up your profile and preferences
3. **Choose AI Mode**: Select between cloud API or on-device AI
4. **Start Chatting**: Begin with a simple conversation to test features
5. **Explore Features**: Try creating a Flow, setting up the Dock, or using the code editor

### First Steps
- **Chat with Buddy**: Ask "What can you help me with?" to get personalized suggestions
- **Create Your First Flow**: Start with a simple project like "Plan my week"
- **Set Up Development Environment**: Connect your coding tools and repositories
- **Explore Templates**: Use built-in templates for common tasks and projects

### Tips for Success
- **Start Small**: Begin with basic features and gradually explore advanced capabilities
- **Customize Your Experience**: Set up AI personas and preferences that match your needs
- **Join the Community**: Connect with other Buddy users for tips and collaboration
- **Keep Learning**: Buddy grows with you - explore new features as they're added
